The tram rattles past at 7:47, a metallic heartbeat that marks the hour. A woman in a coral cardigan steps aboard, a paper bag of soda bread tucked under her arm, the scent of rosemary drifting through the carriage. I inhale, the city’s bakery chorus filling the air. Outside, a copper beech glows bronze in the late‑summer light, its leaves whispering the day's quiet promise.
