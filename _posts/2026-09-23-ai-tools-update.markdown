Grok 4.7 landed, and yeah, it’s trained on SpaceX and Starlink data—talk about niche fuel. At 2.1 trillion parameters, it’s a beast for coding, and you can actually use it in Cursor or via the xAI API right now.

On the open‑source front, Xiaomi dropped MiMo‑V2.6 under an MIT licence. That’s almost unheard of for a model this capable. It’s quick, cheap to run, and it’s already punching above its weight on the leaderboards.

DigitalOcean finally opened Managed Agents to everyone. If you’ve ever hated babysitting agent infrastructure, this is your ticket: just point your harness at their MCP gateway and let them handle the compute and tool connections.

Weftgate nudged to 0.3.0. It’s the little “second brain” helper for coding agents that keeps track of what matters, what’s been decided, and whether your changes actually make sense. Quietly useful.

All in all, a packed week for AI tooling.
