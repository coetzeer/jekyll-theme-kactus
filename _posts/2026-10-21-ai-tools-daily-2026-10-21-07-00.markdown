## Krea 3 — Image Editing Model at $0.06/Edit, Trainable on Your Style

**Category:** Other (Image editing model)  
**Type:** Commercial (SaaS, API)  
**Released:** October 15, 2026  
**Source:** Krea AI, Versely, Glitchwire, OrcaRouter

Krea 3 edits existing photos — it doesn't generate from scratch. Native 2K resolution, most edits under 8 seconds. The differentiator: it's the first trainable image editor. You can fine-tune it on your visual style. Benchmarks put it in second place for image editing.

$0.06 per 2K image via API. This is positioned as production-grade tooling for commercial teams, not a creative toy.

## NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL — 120B Open Multimodal MoE for Always-On Agents

**Category:** Other (Multimodal frontier model, open weights)  
**Type:** Open (permissive, NVIDIA Open Model License)  
**Released:** October 15, 2026  
**Source:** NVIDIA Blog, MarkTechPost, Tech-Insider

120B total parameters, 12B active (MoE). Vision + language, 1M token context, NVFP4 quantization so it runs on a single DGX Spark. Built for always-on multimodal agents. 5x faster than prior Nemotron models. Open weights on Hugging Face and build.nvidia.com.

Part of an expanded family — the Bolt variant below targets local deployment.

## NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Bolt 74B — Hybrid Mamba-Transformer for Local Agentic Work

**Category:** Other (Multimodal hybrid model, open weights)  
**Type:** Open (permissive, NVIDIA Open Model License)  
**Released:** October 15, 2026  
**Source:** NVIDIA Research, Artificial Analysis

74B total, 9B active (MoE hybrid Mamba-Transformer). Vision-language multimodal. Designed to run everyday agentic tasks locally on RTX 5090 / DGX Spark (24GB VRAM). ~84 tokens/sec. Open weights.

This is the "run it on your desk" companion to Super VL. Technical report published same day.

## OpenAI Decisions API — Model/Tool/Action Routing in Public Beta

**Category:** Other (Decision/routing API for agents)  
**Type:** Commercial (SaaS, API)  
**Released:** October 6, 2026 (public beta)  
**Source:** OpenAI Developer Community, MarkTechPost, OrcaRouter

Lets your app choose the right model, tool, or action in near real-time. Returns typed structured outputs ~10x faster than GPT-6 Luna for routing decisions. $0.10 per 1M input tokens. Integrates with Responses API. Playground available.

This is the "traffic cop" layer for agentic systems — deciding what to call before you call it.
