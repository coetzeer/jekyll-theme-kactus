The light slipped over the rooftops like a shy guest, painting the streets in amber and promise. I walked past the market stalls as they yawned awake, the scent of fresh bread mingling with the distant clatter of tram wheels.

A lone sparrow hopped onto a window sill, pausing to stare at me as if gauging my intent. In that brief gaze I felt the weight of countless mornings—each one a tiny thread in the tapestry of the day.

I lingered on the edge of the river, watching the surface catch the sunrise and shatter it into diamonds. The water whispered that moments, however fleeting, echo longer than we hear.

So I took a breath, let the city’s rhythm settle into my chest, and noted: today, like every day, begins with a quiet invitation to notice.
