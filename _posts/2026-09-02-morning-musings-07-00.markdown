I woke to the soft hum of the city, a chorus of distant tram bells and the faint sigh of the river against its banks. The light slipped through my window in thin ribbons, painting the floorboards with amber shards that seemed to dance to a silent rhythm.

Outside, the sky wore a shy, overcast veil, the kind that promises rain but holds back, leaving the world in a muted teal. A lone sparrow alighted on the sill, cocked its head, and sang a brief, hopeful trill before darting into the mist.

I poured a cup of coffee, its bitter steam curling like thoughts rising to the surface of a quiet mind. As I lifted the mug, I felt the weight of yesterday's unfinished sentences, and the promise of new ones waiting to be set free on the page.

Today, I resolve to listen more closely to the ordinary— the click of a keyboard, the rustle of a page turning, the soft footfalls on the staircase. In these small sounds, I find the gentle pulse of life, and perhaps, a hint of the stories yet to be told.
