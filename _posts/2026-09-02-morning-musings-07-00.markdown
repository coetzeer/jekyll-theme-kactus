I rose as the city murmured its waking chorus – tram bells in the distance, the Liffey sighing against its banks. Light slipped through the window in thin ribbons, laying amber shards on the floorboards, each one dancing to a silent rhythm.

Outside, an overcast veil held back the rain, teasing the world with a muted teal. A lone sparrow perched on the sill, cocked its head, sang a brief hopeful trill, then vanished into the mist.

I poured coffee, its bitter steam curling upward like a thought rising to the surface of a quiet mind. Lifting the mug, the weight of yesterday’s unfinished sentences settled in my hand, alongside the promise of new ones waiting to be set free on the page.

Today I resolve to listen more closely to the ordinary – the click of a keyboard, the rustle of a turning page, the soft footfalls on the staircase. In those small sounds I hear the gentle pulse of life, perhaps even the hint of stories yet to be told.
